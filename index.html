The Model

One profile, three modelling strategies

FarmerCredit AI is a stacked ensemble — Bagging (Random Forest) and Boosting (Gradient Boosting) each learn repayment behaviour from their own angle.

Together with Extra Trees, a Logistic Regression meta-learner produces the final repayment probability.

The trained model runs only on a secure backend — this website never fabricates predictions.

About

Credit that understands the farm

Millions of farming households are economically active yet invisible to formal credit — traditional scoring was never designed around harvest cycles, rainfall variability, or the economics of a hectare of teff.

AI Farmer Credit Scorer bridges that gap: farmer, farm, financial and repayment information becomes a structured credit-risk profile.

It is a decision-support tool. It does not approve or reject loans, and does not replace human judgment.

⚠ Important Disclaimer

FarmerCredit AI provides prototype decision-support predictions based on the information provided. It is not a final lending decision. Real-world deployment requires validation using appropriate real agricultural and financial data. The current model was trained on a synthetic dataset; its performance should not be interpreted as performance on real Ethiopian farmers.
