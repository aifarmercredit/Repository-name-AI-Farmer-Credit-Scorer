The Model

One profile, three modelling strategies

FarmerCredit AI is a stacked ensemble. A Random Forest (bagging) and a Gradient Boosting model (boosting) each learn repayment behaviour from their own angle.

They are joined — together with Extra Trees — inside a stacking ensemble, where a Logistic Regression meta-learner produces the final estimate of repayment probability.

The trained model (farmer_credit_ai_model.pkl) runs only on a secure backend. The website never executes the model in the browser and never fabricates predictions — every result you see comes from the real API.

About

Credit that understands the farm

Millions of farming households are economically active yet remain invisible to formal credit — not because they are unworthy borrowers, but because traditional scoring was never designed around harvest cycles, rainfall variability, or the economics of a hectare of teff.

AI Farmer Credit Scorer bridges that gap. It converts farmer, farm, financial and repayment information into a structured credit-risk profile using a machine-learning ensemble built for agricultural lending.

It is a decision-support tool: it informs lenders and empowers farmers with a transparent view of their profile. It does not approve or reject loans, and it does not replace human judgment or regulatory processes.

Farmers Microfinance institutions Banks Agricultural cooperatives Agricultural organizations

Important Disclaimer

FarmerCredit AI provides prototype decision-support predictions based on the information provided. It is not a final lending decision. Real-world deployment requires validation using appropriate real agricultural and financial data.

The current model was trained on a synthetic dataset for prototyping purposes. Its measured performance should therefore not be interpreted as performance on real Ethiopian farmers.
